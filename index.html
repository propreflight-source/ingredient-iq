Scan a product, get a real breakdown of what's actually in it — explained simply, nothing hidden.

How this works, honestly: the score comes from a transparent, published rule list (see "how scoring works" below) applied to a product's real ingredient list from Open Beauty Facts, a free, open, crowdsourced database — not a proprietary lab test, and not a medical opinion. Camera scanning uses your browser's real barcode reader (no camera library download, no account, nothing leaves your device except the barcode number sent to look up the product).

How scoring works (the full transparent list)

Hair type

The standard 1–4 typing system used across the haircare industry. This changes what "highly rated" means for you on the Recommendations tab.

Hair color

Color-treated hair reacts differently to sulfates and alcohols — this tunes recommendations too.

Hair conditions

Select anything that applies — this can help tailor what counts as a stronger or weaker concern for you.
